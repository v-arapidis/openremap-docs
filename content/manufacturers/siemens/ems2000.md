## Detection

Three checks, deliberately conservative — this is the **lowest-confidence**
Siemens detector:

1. **Size gate** — exactly 262,144 bytes (256 KB).
2. **Exclusion** — reject if *any* known manufacturer signature appears
   anywhere in the binary.
3. **Header magic** — accept only on the 4-byte header `\xc0\xf0\x68\xa6`.

There is no family signature to look for: the engine would rather miss an
EMS2000 than wrongly claim one. Part numbers follow the `S108xxxxx` format but
live only in the filename — never embedded in the binary.

**Checksums:** no family profile (generic sweep only).
